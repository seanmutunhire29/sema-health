What's inside

Six things Sema does that mainstream health AI does not.

Every one of these is on-device. No cloud round-trip, no English-only fallback.

Fully offline

The Gemma 3n model and the triage engine both run on the phone. After the one-time model install, Sema works in airplane mode forever.

Voice-first, in your language

Tap the mic, speak in Twi, Swahili, Yorùbá, English, or French. On-device speech recognition and synthesis — no audio ever leaves the device.

Triage that runs in milliseconds

A clinician-reviewed rule engine matches symptoms to WHO IMCI guidance deterministically. Gemma rewrites the recommendation in warm prose — it does not decide what to recommend.

Camera + Gemma 3n vision

Tap the camera button to show Sema a wound, a rash, or a medicine label. Gemma 3n's vision encoder describes the photo on-device and feeds it into the same triage flow.

Auditable, every turn

Open the journal on any past conversation and see the exact rule that fired, the severity assigned, the prompt Gemma was given, and the WHO citation behind it.

One device, many people

A family shares one phone. Sema keeps a separate profile, conversation history, and medical context for each person — protected by optional biometric lock.

Why Sema is different

Most health AI is built on three assumptions. Sema rejects all three.

01

"Users have internet."

In rural West and East Africa, signal drops for days at a time. Sema is built so the most important moments — a child's fever at 2 a.m., a kitchen burn during a power cut — work without a cell tower.

02

"Users speak English."

Sema speaks Twi, Swahili, Yorùbá, English, and French. For low-resource languages, user input is translated to English on-device (NLLB-200 ONNX), the deterministic engine runs, and Gemma 3n translates the warm reply back. The clinical safety case stays auditable in English.

03

"The AI should decide."

Sema's LLM drives expression, not triage. By the time Gemma is invoked on a turn, severity, the matched first-aid entry, and the recommendation are already decided by a deterministic engine. Gemma rewrites the prose. A two-stage safety guard (regex blocklist + drug whitelist) runs over every Gemma output before it reaches the user.

Behind the principle

"The LLM drives expression, not triage."

"A health-companion LLM that decides severity, prescribes doses, and answers diagnostic questions on-the-fly is the wrong architecture for medical guidance. The right architecture lets the LLM be warm and lets clinician-reviewed logic be correct." — Architecture/04-ml-and-ai.md, in the Sema repo

Try Sema on your Android.

Sideload the APK on a mid-tier or better Android (4 GB RAM and up). After install, Sema downloads its language models once over Wi-Fi — about 3 GB for English + Swahili — then runs offline forever.
